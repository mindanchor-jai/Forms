What you had, and what became visible

Take one recent moment and look at the difference between having something to contribute and allowing yourself to contribute it.

About 6 minutes. Everything you write stays on this page. Nothing is submitted. Any answer, including “I’m not sure,” is a valid observation.

Choose one moment

Think of a recent time you stayed quiet, kept an idea to yourself, or kept your contribution small.

Separate the two

People usually experience these as one thing. Here they are written apart.

Roughly how much of what you had became visible?
Very littleAll of it

Notice what it seemed to cost

If you had offered the whole of it, what would have come with that? Choose any that apply.
