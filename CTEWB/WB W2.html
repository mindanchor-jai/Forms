Workbook for Article 2 · Confidence Through Evidence

What stood between knowing and saying

Take one moment where you knew more than you said, and look at where the route from knowing to saying narrowed.

About 6 minutes. Everything you write stays on this page. Nothing is submitted. Any answer, including “I’m not sure,” is a valid observation.

Choose one moment

Think of a time you knew the answer or had another way to do it, but it did not come out, or came out weakly.

Follow the route

Knowledge has to travel to your voice. For this moment, mark how each stretch of the route felt.

Knowing itDid you have the answer or idea?
Trusting itDid you trust that what you knew was worth saying?
Saying itDid it come out as quickly and steadily as you knew it?

Notice what was present

Where it narrowed, what was around you? Choose any that apply.

What do you see now?

Not only “am I able?” but a second question. Complete the sentence.

This reflection is yours.

Where do you know more than you usually say, and what makes it hard to bring out loud?
