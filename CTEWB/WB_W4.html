From event to evidence

Take one experience you got through, and look at what it shows about what you could manage.

About 6 minutes. Everything you write stays on this page. Nothing is submitted. Any answer, including “I’m not sure,” is a valid observation.

Choose one experience

Pick something you got through: a hard conversation, a mistake you recovered from, a stretch you carried. One situation, not a period of your life.

Look at what you did

Not how it felt. What you actually did in it.

Connect it to what it shows

An event stays scattered until something connects it to what it shows. Try one sentence.
